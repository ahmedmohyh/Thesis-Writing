The conclusion is where you wrap up your ideas and leave the reader with a strong final impression. It has several key goals:

\begin{itemize}[label=-, topsep=0pt,itemsep=-1ex,partopsep=0ex,parsep=1ex]
    \item Restate the problem statement addressed in the thesis
    \item Summarize your overall arguments or findings
    \item Suggest the key takeaways from your paper
\end{itemize}

The conclusion should be compared with the abstract (if applicable) and the introduction. Similar to the introduction, the conclusion should make up about 5\% of the entire thesis.

Please do not forget to \textbf{check the spelling} of the entire thesis. You need to write your thesis in \textbf{English}. Please use "\textbf{American English}".
